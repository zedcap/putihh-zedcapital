La Villa des Sens

An oasis of calm in bustling Seminyak, La Villa Des Sens is a spacious experience of Balinese luxury in which to relax and feel truly at home.

This contemporary villa is built on 1,000m² of land in a ‘U’ shape, around a tall traditional Balinese Joglo and faces a sparkling 4m x 20m swimming pool surrounded by a lush garden.
