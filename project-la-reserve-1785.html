La Réserve 1785 Private Villas

Thirteen rooms, adults only, and a MICHELIN Key. La Réserve 1785 is a member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World, set in one of Bali’s most desirable neighbourhoods, Pererenan.

A short walk away, its Private Villas Collection adds six three-bedroom villas, each with a private garden, a long pool and luxury amenities. Designed by the hotel’s architect and run by La Réserve, the villas bring five-star hotel services to a tranquil, family-friendly setting.
